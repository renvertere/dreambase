# 🧯 **Template: Troubleshooting Guide**

**Purpose:**  
To document the **diagnosis and resolution of technical issues** in a structured, reusable way — turning one-off errors into repeatable solutions and learning artifacts.

**Why it was made:**  
Troubleshooting is often ad-hoc and undocumented.  
This template ensures every problem you solve becomes **captured knowledge** — showing both _what went wrong_ and _how it was fixed_, in clear technical and layman terms.  
It’s designed to reinforce problem-solving skills, identify recurring root causes, and support continuous improvement.

**When to use:**  
Use this template whenever you encounter an **unexpected error, failure, or anomaly** — whether during experiments, deployments, or day-to-day system operations.  
It’s ideal for building a “known issues” knowledge base or linking root causes to related How-Tos and Atoms.

**Outcome:**  
A structured troubleshooting record that includes:

- 🎯 **Goal** — what you were trying to do
- ⚠️ **Problem (Technical)** — exact error message or failure
- 🤔 **Problem (Layman)** — simple explanation for broader understanding
- 🌍 **Analogy** — conceptual model for why the issue occurred
- 🔍 **Investigation Areas** — likely root causes to check
- 🛠️ **Solution Steps** — clear, actionable resolution
- ✅ **Verification** — evidence that the issue is resolved
- 🔗 **Related Notes** — links to How-Tos, Atoms, or similar cases
