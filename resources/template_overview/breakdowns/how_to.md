# 🛠️ **Template: How-To Guide**

**Purpose:**  
To document a **repeatable technical procedure or workflow** — step-by-step, with clear prerequisites, commands, and verification steps.

**Why it was made:**  
How-Tos turn **knowledge into action**.  
They bridge the gap between _conceptual notes_ (Atoms) and _practical exercises_ by providing a structured, verified process that can be followed reliably across systems and environments.

**When to use:**  
Use this template whenever you’re performing a **task that could be repeated later** — installations, configurations, deployments, troubleshooting, etc.  
It ensures every procedure is clear, auditable, and linked to the concepts behind it.

**Outcome:**  
A self-contained, executable guide that provides:

- 🧾 **Summary** — what the guide accomplishes
- 🧰 **Prerequisites** — tools, permissions, or setup required
- 🧑‍💻 **Steps** — ordered, numbered instructions with code snippets
- ✅ **Verification** — confirmation checks or test outputs
- 🔗 **Related Notes** — conceptual or supporting materials
