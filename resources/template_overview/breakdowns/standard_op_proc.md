# 📋 **Template: Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)**

**Purpose:**  
To define a **repeatable, controlled process** that ensures technical or operational tasks are performed **safely, consistently, and in compliance** with policy or standard requirements.

**Why it was made:**  
SOPs formalize _best practice_ and _accountability_.  
They capture the **exact steps**, **responsible roles**, **risks**, and **verification criteria** for critical operations — reducing human error and ensuring traceability during audits or incident reviews.

**When to use:**  
Use this template whenever a procedure must be **replicated exactly or verified for compliance** — e.g., database backup and restore, system hardening, security patching, or failover testing.  
It is ideal for production operations, regulated environments, and QA-reviewed workflows.

**Outcome:**  
A formalized, auditable procedure containing:

- 🧾 **Purpose & Scope** — why it exists and where it applies
- 🧰 **Prerequisites** — required tools, roles, and systems
- 🧑‍💻 **Procedure** — step-by-step actions with code or commands
- ✅ **Verification** — evidence of successful execution
- ⚠️ **Safety / Risks** — identified hazards and mitigations
- 📅 **Roles & Responsibilities** — who owns and performs it
- ⏱ **Benchmark / Time Tracking** — procedural timing for optimization
- 📝 **Revision History** — version control for procedural updates
