# 🎓 **Template: Course README**

**Purpose:**  
To summarize an entire **course, learning path, or study track** — showing what it covers, what you’ll learn, and how it connects to your notes.

**Why it was made:**  
Course READMEs act as the **anchor point** for structured learning.  
They give each course or certification a single, centralized entry — tracking progress, linking key concepts, and tying all related Atoms, How-Tos, and Exercises together.

**When to use:**  
Use it at the **start of any new course or training series** (e.g., “Oracle SQL Bootcamp,” “Physical Wellness Foundations,” “Home Architecture 101”).  
It helps organize modules, track completion, and link supporting materials.

**Outcome:**  
A consistent, navigable course summary that includes:

- 🔗 **Resources** — official course links and index
- 🎯 **Learning Outcomes** — what the learner will achieve
- 🔖 **Key Sections** — major parts of the course
- 📅 **Progress Tracker** — checklist of modules or milestones
- 🧠 **Linked Notes** — connects to deeper topics in the vault
