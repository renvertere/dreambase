# 🗣️ **Template: Meeting Transcript**

**Purpose:**  
To document the **key details, discussions, and follow-up actions** from a meeting — capturing who attended, what was discussed, and what decisions or next steps were agreed upon.

**Why it was made:**  
Meetings often generate critical insights, commitments, and next steps that get lost in chat threads or calls.  
This template transforms raw conversation into a **structured, timestamped record**, ensuring continuity, accountability, and context for future sessions.

**When to use:**  
Use this template after **any formal or recurring meeting** — project stand-ups, design reviews, strategy sessions, or planning calls.  
It’s particularly useful for distributed teams or for building an audit trail of decisions.

**Outcome:**  
A consistent, searchable meeting record with:

- 🔗 **Participants** — who attended and their roles
- 🎯 **Goals** — what the meeting intended to achieve
- 🔖 **Discussion Topics** — major points and insights
- 📅 **Action Items / Next Steps** — decisions, owners, and due dates
- 🧭 **Next Meeting** — scheduled follow-up and draft agenda
