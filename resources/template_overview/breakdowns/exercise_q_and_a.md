# **Template Pair: Exercise Q & A**

**Purpose:**  
To capture **practical, test-driven learning** — from the question or task (_Exercise Q_) through to the executed solution and reflection (_Exercise A_).  
Together, they record **hands-on practice, troubleshooting, and verification** in a repeatable format.

**Why they were made:**  
Exercises turn theory into _experience_.  
The **Q** note defines the requirement — what must be done.  
The **A** note documents what was actually done,
what happened, and what was learned.  
This separation enforces behavioural checks
and lets you version, compare, or re-run exercises cleanly without losing traceability.

**When to use:**  
Use them whenever testing a concept from an Atom or following a How-To guide.  
They’re ideal for certification prep, repeatable lab work, or documenting variations between systems.

**Outcome:**  
A complete learning record that includes:

- 🎯 **Exercise Q** — defines the task or challenge
- 🧑‍💻 **Exercise A** — captures commands, observations, and outcomes
- 👁️ **Troubleshooting log** — errors, warnings, deviations
- ✅ **Evaluation** — success criteria and takeaways
- 🔗 **Related links** — connects to relevant How-Tos, Logs, and Atoms
