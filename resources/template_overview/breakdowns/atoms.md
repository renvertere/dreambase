# ⚛️ **Template: Atom Note**

**Purpose:**  
To capture a _single, self-contained technical concept_ — small enough to stand alone, but complete enough to teach.

**Why it was made:**  
Atoms are the _building blocks_ of the DreamBase knowledge base. Each one defines one concept clearly (like `ROWID`, `Mathe`, or `ZFS snapshot`) so it can be reused, linked, and explained without repetition.

**When to use:**  
Use an Atom when documenting a _core term, idea, or mechanism_ that appears in multiple guides or exercises.

**Outcome:**  
A concise, linkable note containing:

- 💡 **Core Idea** — formal definition or key statement
- 🧠 **Explanation** — plain-language version 
- 🧪 **Example** — command or snippet
- 🧭 **Use Cases** — when or why it matters
- 🔗 **Relations** — links to related Atoms, Exercises, or How-Tos
