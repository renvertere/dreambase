# 🎞️ **Template: Video → Atom Index Creator**

**Purpose:**  
To convert a **raw video transcript** into a structured list of **Atom-worthy concepts** — creating a clear, time-coded index of key ideas that can later be expanded into full knowledge notes.

**Why it was made:**  
Video content often contains dense clusters of teachable concepts, but they’re easy to lose in unstructured transcripts.  
This template provides a **systematic way to extract and label conceptual nodes**, enabling faster ingestion of multimedia learning into your atomic knowledge base.

**When to use:**  
Use this template after **watching or transcribing any educational or technical video** (tutorials, lectures, conference talks, etc.).  
It’s ideal for building your initial Atom backlog before detailed writing begins.

**Outcome:**  
A concise, timestamped mapping of the video’s knowledge flow, including:

- 🗂️ **Atom Index Table** — timestamped list of identified concepts
