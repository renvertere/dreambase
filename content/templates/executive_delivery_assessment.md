# 12. Prompt for Use

> I am going to provide you with a proposed initiative, technical change, operational improvement, or solution objective.
> Convert it into an Executive Delivery Assessment.
>
> The purpose of the assessment is to demonstrate the timeline, effort, bill of materials, dependencies, risks, and decision requirements needed to achieve the stated outcome.
>
> Keep the output executive-facing. Avoid unnecessary technical depth unless it directly affects cost, timeline, effort, risk, or dependencies.

## Guidelines

* State the objective in clear business language.
* Identify the decision required from leadership.
* Define the outcome to be achieved.
* Summarize the delivery timeline using practical delivery phases.
* Estimate effort by role or team.
* Produce a bill of materials covering infrastructure, licences, hardware, software, services, and training.
* Identify dependencies and critical path items.
* Highlight risks and constraints in business-facing terms.
* Assess delivery readiness.
* Provide a final recommendation with conditions and immediate next actions.
